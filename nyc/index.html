How to play

Every day you get 5 New York City places. Tap where you think each one is.

Closer guesses score more (up to 100 per round). Misses hurt most when other landmarks are closer to your tap than the answer; in quieter areas, being a mile or two off is fine. Later rounds are worth more: ×1, ×1, ×2, ×3, ×3 — 1,000 points max.

Drag to move the map, pinch or scroll to zoom. Street names are shown; place names are not.

A new challenge unlocks at midnight your local time.
