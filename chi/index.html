How to play

Every day you get 5 Chicago places. Tap where you think each one is.

Closer guesses score more (up to 100 per round). Later rounds are worth more: ×1, ×1, ×2, ×3, ×3 — 1,000 points max.

Drag to move the map, pinch or scroll to zoom. Street names are shown; place names are not.

A new challenge unlocks at midnight your local time.
